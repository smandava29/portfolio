03 How I work

A clear path from
problem to product.

Turn signals into a problem worth solving

Start with the problem, then understand the system around it.

I use customer, business, and operational signals to frame the real opportunity before committing to a solution. At iPowerUp, that means mapping how device behavior, mobile experiences, and data flows can become monitoring, insights, and recommendations people will trust.

  • Product discovery & requirements
  • Data definitions & integration mapping
  • Customer and operational signals

Make trade-offs visible

Build the roadmap around value, feasibility, and evidence.

I evaluate options—from RAG-backed analytics and recommendation experiences to platform capabilities and workflow improvements—against dependencies, scalability, cost, and the evidence needed to know they are working.

  • Roadmap & backlog prioritization
  • AI product and technical trade-offs
  • Success measures & release criteria

Ship with confidence

Give teams a clear path from scope to product readiness.

I turn the decision into scoped work, align teams around it, and stay close to testing, data quality, risks, and release readiness until the product is ready to learn from.

  • Cross-functional delivery
  • Integration testing & readiness
  • Metrics, risks & stakeholder alignment
